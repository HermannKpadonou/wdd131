site Plan - Ann Nielsen Private College of Excellence

Site Name

Ann Nielsen Private College of Excellence

This name reflects the excellence and private approach of the institution while honoring the name of its founder.

Logo

The logo represents the identity of Ann Nielsen Private College of Excellence. It is a modern and professional design that reflects the values of the institution.

Site Purpose

The website aims to showcase Ann Nielsen Private College of Excellence, highlight its programs, pedagogical approach, team, and facilitate contact with the institution.

Scenarios

  • A parent seeks information about the school's programs:
    The site should provide clear and detailed descriptions of the various programs, admission criteria, and tuition fees.
  • A prospective student wishes to contact the school:
    The site should offer a simple and effective contact form, as well as the institution's contact details.
  • A visitor wants to see photos of the school:
    The site should include a gallery section with high-quality images showcasing the school's facilities, activities, and events.
  • A people want to donate for his school:
    The site should provide a donation section with clear instructions on how to contribute.

Color Scheme

Primary Color: #013883 (Dark Blue) - Used for titles, call-to-action buttons, and important elements.
Secondary Color: #A6944B (Gold/Tan) - Used for accents, highlights, and potentially some background elements.
Accent Color: #3E5D7A (Steel Blue) - Used for borders, separator elements, and potentially secondary text.

Typography

Headings: Roboto, semi-bold weight

Body Text: Open Sans, regular weight

Site Pages

  • Homepage: General overview of the school, mission, values, and call to action (contact, registration).
  • About: Detailed description of programs, pedagogy, teaching staff, testimonials.
  • Gallery & Contact: Photo gallery illustrating daily life at the school,Contact details, contact form, access map.

Reason for Subject Choice

This project is chosen for its socially responsible aspect. By creating a website for Ann Nielsen Private College of Excellence, I contribute to its visibility and mission of inclusive education.
